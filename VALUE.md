# Why OpenTarget Matters

## The Problem
Targets are where biology meets chemistry — the protein, gene, or pathway a drug will intervene on. Picking the right target determines whether a $500M development program produces a transformative therapy or a Phase III failure. Yet target selection today is often driven by *ad hoc* literature review, individual scientist preference, and whatever target-validation evidence happens to be top-of-mind.

## What OpenTarget Does
Turns target validation into a structured, reproducible process. A query for a target-disease pair returns a dossier with bulk and single-cell expression evidence, available chemistry (with potency and selectivity), AlphaFold structure quality, clinical-variant context from ClinVar, and an integrated go / no-go recommendation. Every claim is sourced.

## Who Benefits
- Biotech target-validation teams
- Academic labs writing R01 / RPG grant applications
- Pharma BD teams evaluating in-licensing candidates
- VCs running technical diligence

## Why Now
Open Targets, Human Protein Atlas, single-cell atlases (CellxGene, Tabula Sapiens), AlphaFold DB, ChEMBL, and ClinVar are all in the public domain with structured APIs. The integration problem — pulling them into a coherent dossier with consistent reasoning — is exactly what LLMs solve.

## The Bigger Picture
Wrong-target Phase III failures are the single most expensive event in pharma. Reducing them by even a small fraction has industry-scale leverage. Lowering the cost and effort of high-quality target validation directly improves R&D productivity — and improves the odds that real medicines reach the patients who need them.
